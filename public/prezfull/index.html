03 / MATERIAL HONESTY

THREE MATERIALS. THREE RESPONSIBILITIES.

We refuse superficial cladding and synthetic laminates. Every texture in a Prezfull structure is structural, honest to its geological or forestry origin, and tactile to human touch.

01 / THE GROUND

Board-Marked Concrete

Density: 2,400 kg/m³ · Thermal Inertia

Cast in-situ against rough-sawn pine formwork, providing an unadorned structural anchor that weathers with dignity.

02 / THE TOUCH

Quarter-Sawn White Oak

FSC Certified · Natural Plant Oil

Hand-finished joinery, acoustic wall slats, and brass-inlaid thresholds designed to warm every point of human contact.

03 / THE LIGHT

Honed Roman Travertine

Open-Pore Stone · Zero Synthetic Glaze

Diffuses low-angle seasonal daylight deep into interior volumes while buffering ambient acoustics.
